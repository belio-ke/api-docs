SMS templates are reusable message formats that let you send structured,
reliable messages by referencing a template instead of composing raw text.
Template content uses `{{placeholder}}` syntax, and placeholders may be named
(e.g. `{{name}}`) or positional (e.g. `{{1}}`).

SMS templates are categorized into three types:
1. **Marketing** (`MARKETING`): Promotional messages, special offers, and event announcements.
2. **Utility** (`UTILITY`): Transactional information such as order confirmations, appointment reminders, and account updates.
3. **Authentication** (`AUTHENTICATION`): Verification codes and one-time passwords.

### Template scope and approval

Each template has a `scope` that determines the sender policy it belongs to:

- A `TeamMessaging` template has a `policy` of `Standard` or
  `PoliticalCampaign`.
- A `ManagedService` template has a `key` identifying its Belio-managed sender.

Approval status is independent of template `status`. A template must be
`Active` to send. Depending on the sender and scope, an approval status of
`APPROVED` or `FLAGGED` may be accepted; political campaign sends require
`APPROVED` only. See [SMS sending](/messaging/sms/send#sender-and-template-eligibility)
for the complete compatibility rules.

### Belio-managed Senders and Approval

When creating a template, you can link it to a Belio-managed sender. Such
templates use `scope: { "type": "ManagedService", "key": "<key>" }` and can only
be sent through a Belio-managed service with the matching ownership key. Belio
also provides pre-approved templates; eligibility still depends on the
template's scope, approval status, and the sender used.
